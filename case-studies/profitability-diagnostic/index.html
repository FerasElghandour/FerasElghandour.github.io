Acquisition Economics & Profitability

Shopify and Meta performance, contribution economics, offers, retention and cash constraints.

01 / The problem

The decision behind the work.

The client wanted specific decisions about profitability and scale. Store sales, advertising results and subscription activity described different parts of the business. A useful diagnosis had to connect them without treating revenue growth or platform ROAS as proof of profit.

The delivery constraint

The available history was short, acquisition cohorts were mixed, and recurring revenue could obscure the economics of the first order. That made an immediate scaling recommendation premature.

02 / Delivery approach

Connect the economics before scaling.

Make the unit economics explicit

The diagnostic separated product and fulfilment costs from advertising cost, then considered renewal value as a separate assumption. It also distinguished operating performance from financing-related cash outflows. This gave the client a clearer basis for deciding what could be scaled.

Connect acquisition with the offer

The analysis distinguished cold traffic from retargeting and reactivation. It compared page destinations and considered pack size, purchase cadence and one-time versus subscription choices as testable offer decisions. Small purchase counts were treated as exploratory evidence.

Protect the value after the first purchase

Retention recommendations covered early cancellation, pre-renewal communication and failed-payment recovery. The proposed execution plan sequenced these alongside acquisition tests, with human approval and minimum evidence requirements before material budget or offer changes.
