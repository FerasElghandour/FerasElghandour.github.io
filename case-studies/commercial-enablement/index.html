Commercial Systems & Sales Enablement

My role
Commercial systems strategy, sales-journey design, leadership guidance and hands-on operator enablement, with specialist implementation support.
Operating context
A B2B business coordinating leadership, sales staff and marketing delivery.
Scope
Market research, account qualification, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, HubSpot working practices and follow-up ownership.

01 / The problem

The decision behind the work.

Commercial research and new sales tools only create value when they become part of daily work. The engagement needed to connect strategic priorities with account selection, follow-up, meeting availability and clear ownership across the sales team.

The delivery constraint

Existing relationships and offline opportunities needed to coexist with new prospecting. Operators had different levels of readiness, and unqualified automation risked contacting people already in active conversation.

02 / Delivery approach

Connect research, tools and daily work.

Translate strategy into a sales process

Working discussions connected the initial offer, prospect criteria, outreach, meeting booking and follow-up. The approach also accounted for existing offline opportunities, so the CRM process could represent the business beyond newly generated digital leads.

Work with the people using the tools

In individual sessions, I guided staff through professional-profile updates and the prospecting workflow, explained how CRM activity and notes should inform follow-up, and addressed the need to exclude existing conversations from inappropriate outreach sequences. Specialist configuration and wider rollout were collaborative workstreams.

Sequence the rollout around adoption

Leadership reviews covered coordinator responsibility, calendar availability, a focused initial offer and a staged sales pilot. The discussion also separated business revenue from a demonstrated return on the transformation work. Consistent usage and a reliable feedback loop remained essential next steps.

03 / Results

What the engagement established.

  • Research put to use

    A client participant confirmed reading the research and applying it in a strategic industry discussion.

  • Direct enablement delivered

    Two individual working-session transcripts record guidance on profiles, prospecting and CRM practices.

  • Adoption priorities clarified

    Leadership review addressed operating ownership, offer focus and a staged pilot; wider adoption was still in progress.

Evidence and measurement scope

Retained individual enablement-session transcripts and a leadership-review transcript containing direct client feedback. People, company details, account lists and internal financial figures are omitted.

The records support research use, direct enablement and commercial process design. They do not establish a completed autonomous sales platform, a fully adopted rollout or incremental revenue attributable to the engagement.
