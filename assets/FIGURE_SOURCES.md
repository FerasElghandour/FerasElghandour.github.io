## Meaning and limits

- **Capacity:** `capacity-followup-mobile.svg` redraws all four comparisons from the [256-model summary](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/recursive-network-capacity-study/blob/aab76bfeb9a42eab6312d5c3f008c4d41a56029a/results/followup-v1/summary.json). Thirty-two paired seeds per comparison; plotted effects and intervals convert accuracy fractions to percentage points. Intervals are descriptive and pointwise; p values use the declared Holm correction. Previously inspected datasets; no universal topology advantage. The original 1,120-run explorer is historical.
- **Generative:** `generative-paired-mobile.svg` shows all six [pipeline accuracies](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/conditional-handwriting-generation/blob/df3e83573328ac64f9b97ffea683a1939eb8e778/results/comparison-v1/summary.json): cGAN 95.24%, 81.65%, 82.87%; DDPM 96.24%, 95.81%, 96.52%. Each pipeline trains a fresh classifier on 10,000 synthetic digits, then tests on real MNIST. Means ± sample SD: 86.59% ± 7.52 percentage points and 96.19% ± 0.36 percentage points. Fifty cGAN epochs on CPU versus forty DDPM epochs on MPS are unequal compute. Three pipelines per model are descriptive. The full-size source figure also reports the fresh 5,000/5,000 FID protocol; FID is not recomputed here. Seed-101 sample grids are illustrative, not a replacement for the six-seed evaluation. See the repository for portable checkpoints and measured MPS continuation drift.
- **Tennis:** `tennis-robust.png` and `tennis-robust-mobile.svg` are copied unchanged from the [audited ten-run campaign](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/tennis-reinforcement-learning/blob/68ac19549f21a906783c5bffb2561002621b97bc/results/robust-v2/RESULTS.md). Five new training seeds per pipeline, exactly 40,000 environment interactions each. Validation-only checkpoint selection; 100 final episodes per trained policy across two new environment seeds. DDPG mean ± sample SD 1.1008 ± 1.1304, TD3 0.6427 ± 1.0881; 3/5 and 2/5 policy means reach 0.5. Neither meets the four-of-five target. The unit of replication is five trained policies per pipeline; nested episodes are not independent trainings. Equal interactions do not equalize architecture, noise, optimizer or compute. Standard TD3, no algorithm novelty claim. Previous six-run and historical actor records remain separate.
- **Braking:** `braking-at-speed.png` and `braking-at-speed-mobile.svg` are copied unchanged from [the 66-run at-speed campaign](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/autonomous-braking-simulation/blob/916b4cf760a541e1c9f67645fb464d0cb91e7816/results/at-speed-v2/RESULTS.md). Eleven conditions × three physics seeds × two policies. Both pass 27/33 runs with six contacts; 15 m/s short-gap and severe-gap cases fail all repeats. Verified 10/15 m/s cruise precedes scripted kinematic targets. Friction parameters, delayed/noisy observations and dropout broaden the simulation conditions without establishing calibrated weather, real traffic or road safety. Signed collision-hull clearance uses physical orientation and pinned model bounding boxes; it differs from the earlier vehicle-reference distance. Four development runs and earlier nine from-rest cases remain separate. Many deterministic repeats are identical; seeds are not independent real-world deployments.
- **Composition:** homepage table uses the [research report](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/recursive-composition-benchmark/blob/b31b8f04089f54c91e40506e45dfdff7ccac34e6/REPORT.md), nine conditions × five seeds, all 45 runs reproduced. Held-out-pair accuracy uses mean ± sample SD. The known-algebra baseline has an information advantage. The balanced staged hypothesis failed on this four-state generated task; broad reasoning or cross-domain transfer is not established.
- **Fusion:** `fusion-comparison.png` and `fusion-mobile.svg` retain the original [release evaluation](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/sensor-fusion-policy-engine/blob/main/validation/release-20261017/results.json). Mean F1 across three seeds, 1,000 synthetic missions each: deterministic 0.9416, learned 0.9298. Dots show seeds; these are not operational sensor metrics.
- **Maintenance:** `maintenance-outcomes.png` and `maintenance-mobile.svg` retain the original [replay data](https://github.com/FerasElghandour/predictive-maintenance/blob/main/docs/data.json): 29 timely warnings, one early intervention, five missed observed windows and 65 histories censored before the danger window. The threshold was selected on validation. These are observed-window outcomes from historical simulated engine records.

## Mechanism schematics and historical assets

`topology-comparison.svg` and its mobile variant depict the implemented eight-group seed-zero trees: balanced depth 3, random 4, skewed 7. They show feature encoders, concatenating merge blocks and classifier paths. Satimage uses nine groups. These are mechanisms, not experimental outcomes.

`shared-policy-mechanism.svg` and its mobile variant describe the DDPG branch’s shared actor, one local critic, shared replay, 128-transition minibatches and target update tau 0.01. TD3 uses two local critics, clipped targets and smoothing, delayed actor/target updates and tau 0.005; the older schematic does not depict that branch. The critic processes one player at a time; the diagram does not depict a centralized critic.

`cgan-pipeline.svg` and its mobile variant describe the MLP cGAN branch: G 110→128→256→512→784 and D 794→512→256→1, using a ten-value class embedding. Labels now cover seeds 101/202/303 and per-pipeline utility evaluation. The separate conditional residual U-Net DDPM branch uses forty epochs and is not represented as an MLP or convolutional cGAN.
